Privacy policy - rclone-jellyfin

Last updated: October 17, 2026

  • rclone-jellyfin is a personal app used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.
  • Google Drive data is accessed only to list, read and store the owner's own files for playback on the owner's own media server. Access tokens are stored only on that server.
  • No data is collected from anyone else, and no data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
